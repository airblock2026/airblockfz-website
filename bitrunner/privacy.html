Privacy Policy — Bit Runner

Last updated: 2026-07-29 · Developer: Air Block L.L.C-FZ (Meydan Free Zone, Dubai, U.A.E.) · Contact: info@airblockfz.com

Bit Runner (the "Game") is an arcade running game. We designed it to work without accounts and without collecting personal information. This policy explains exactly what the Game does and does not do with data.

1. Information we do NOT collect

  • We do not ask you to create an account, sign in, or provide a name, email address, or phone number.
  • We do not collect contacts, photos, files, location, microphone, or camera data. The Game requests none of these permissions.
  • We do not operate our own servers that store user data, and we never sell personal information.

2. Information stored on your device only

Your game progress is saved locally on your device and is never transmitted to us:

  • Current stage, collected coins, and high score
  • Items and skills you obtained
  • Settings (language, difficulty, music and sound on/off)

This data is kept in the app's local storage (localStorage and Android SharedPreferences). Uninstalling the Game deletes it permanently. You can also clear it from your device's app settings ("Clear storage").

3. Advertising

The Game shows rewarded video ads (to revive or to obtain items) and an interstitial ad after the bonus mini-game, served by Google AdMob. To serve and measure ads, Google may process your device's advertising identifier and standard technical data such as device model, OS version, coarse region, and ad interaction events.

  • Google's practices: How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services
  • You can reset or delete your advertising ID at any time in your device settings (Android: Settings → Privacy → Ads).
  • Where required (EEA, UK), a consent message is presented before personalized ads are shown.

4. In-app purchases

The Game offers optional one-time in-app purchases (items and special moves). All payments are processed by Google Play Billing. We never see, receive, or store your payment details — card numbers, billing address, and Google account information never reach us or the Game. The Game receives only a purchase confirmation from Google Play, used solely to deliver the purchased item to your device; the item is then stored locally like any other progress data. Refunds, purchase history, and payment disputes are handled by Google Play under its own terms.

5. Network connections

The Game's terrain is generated from real cryptocurrency price charts. To do this, the Game reads public market data from public endpoints operated by Bybit and Binance. These requests contain no personal information and no account credentials — only anonymous public price and liquidation data. This data is used solely to draw the game world.

The Game is entertainment software. It does not provide financial, investment, or trading advice, and it does not let you buy, sell, or hold any cryptocurrency or other asset. In-game coins have no monetary value and cannot be exchanged for money or crypto.

6. Children

The Game is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If you believe a child has provided personal information, contact us and we will address it.

7. Data retention and your rights

Because we do not collect or store personal data on any server, we hold nothing to retrieve, correct, or delete. All Game data lives on your device and is fully under your control (uninstall or clear storage). For questions about advertising data, please refer to Google's policy linked above.

8. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and revise the "Last updated" date above.

  • 계정 생성·로그인을 요구하지 않으며 이름, 이메일, 전화번호를 수집하지 않습니다.

2. 기기에만 저장되는 정보

게임 진행 정보는 이용자 기기에만 저장되며 외부로 전송되지 않습니다.

  • 현재 스테이지, 수집한 코인, 최고 점수

이 정보는 앱 내부 저장소(localStorage 및 안드로이드 SharedPreferences)에 보관되며, 앱을 삭제하면 함께 삭제됩니다. 기기의 앱 설정에서 "저장공간 지우기"로도 삭제할 수 있습니다.

3. 광고

본 게임은 Google AdMob을 통해 보상형 동영상 광고(부활·아이템 획득)와 보너스 미니게임 종료 후 전면 광고를 게재합니다. 광고 게재·측정을 위해 Google이 기기의 광고 ID와 기기 모델·OS 버전·대략적 지역·광고 상호작용 기록 등 기술 정보를 처리할 수 있습니다.

  • Google 처리 방침: Google 서비스를 사용하는 앱의 정보 이용 방식
  • 광고 ID는 기기 설정에서 언제든지 재설정·삭제할 수 있습니다(안드로이드: 설정 → 개인정보 보호 → 광고).
  • 법령상 필요한 지역(EEA·영국)에서는 맞춤 광고 게재 전 동의 창을 표시합니다.
